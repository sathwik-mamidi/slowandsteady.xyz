How to play SlowAndSteady 2

The goal

SlowAndSteady 2 is an idle generosity game. Your leaderboard score grows from what you give, not what you keep. The main loop is simple: wait, build a gift, donate it, then become stronger for the next gift.

How gifts work

  • Your wallet grows over time from your current boost.
  • The next gift is roughly half of your current wallet plus your time-based growth.
  • When you donate, your wallet resets to zero.
  • The player you donate to receives the gift in their wallet.
  • Your total donated increases permanently, which improves future gifts.

When you can donate

The donation progress bar shows how close your next gift is to being strong enough. When it reaches 100%, the Time to donate card turns ready and donation buttons become useful.

Growth titles

Your title is based on total donated: Seed, Sprout, Sapling, Tree, Grove, Forest, and Ancient. The growth track shows each milestone and how far you are from the next one.

Ways to donate

  • Use Leaderboard search to find any player and donate directly.
  • Use Donate Random when you want to gift someone quickly.
  • Donate to friends or recent players from the Received and Sent sections.
  • Join or create guilds. Guild donations split across the other members.

Strategy

Small early gifts help you grow quickly. Later, patience matters more because stronger players need longer waits to make meaningful gifts. The best long-term progress comes from donating regularly instead of only saving wallet balance.
